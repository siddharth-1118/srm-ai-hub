Foreign Visiting Professors
Foreign Visiting Professors play a vital role in SRM’s internationalization strategy by bringing diverse academic insights and global best practices into our classrooms and research initiatives. Through lectures, collaborative research, seminars, and student mentorship, they enhance the academic depth and global relevance of our programs. These professors are invited based on their academic credentials, research impact, and alignment with SRM’s vision for global engagement.

Foreign Adjunct Professors
International Adjunct Professors at SRM play an integral role in bridging global expertise with local academic excellence. These distinguished academicians and industry experts are associated with SRM over extended periods, contributing remotely or through periodic visits. Their involvement includes co-teaching, research supervision, joint publications, and curriculum design in collaboration with SRM faculty.
