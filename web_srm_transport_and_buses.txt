Transport Booking Instructions for Students
Kindly update the following instructions in Student Bus Booking portal for Transport Booking and it is as follows:
1 ) Possession of Bus Pass ID Card is Mandatory for Students.
2 ) Bus Pass should be shown when authority demands.
3 ) In Case of Bus Pass ID lost, duplicate bus pass provided to students at the Cost of Rs.250/- in Transport Office.
