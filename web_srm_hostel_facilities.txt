Oori (AC/ Non-attached washroom)
Welcome to the Oori Block! Vallal Oori was one of the seven great Tamil philanthropists (Kadai Ezhu Vallalgal) celebrated in ancient Sangam literature. He ruled over Oori Naadu, located in present-day Tamil Nadu. Vallal Oori was renowned for his immense generosity and compassion. His legacy remains a shining example of selflessness and service to others, making him a revered figure in Tamil cultural and historical narratives.
Oori Block is a well-furnished boys’ hostel that offers a pleasant and comfortable living environment. The hostel includes a study hall and indoor games facilities such as carrom, chess, table tennis, and more. The building faces west and is centrally located, comprising a total of Ground plus Nine (G+9) floors. All rooms are air-conditioned and available on a four or five-sharing basis, with common washroom facilities.

N Block
Welcome to N Block, a fully furnished boys’ hostel designed to provide a comfortable, secure, and modern living experience. Featuring a north-facing entrance, the property comprises Ground + 19 floors (G+19) and offers thoughtfully designed accommodation options in both 2-sharing and 3-sharing formats.
All rooms are air-conditioned and come with attached washrooms, ensuring convenience and comfort for residents. The hostel is designed to meet the needs of students and young professionals with a focus on safety, functionality, and quality living.
