At SRMIST, sports are an essential part of holistic education and student life. Guided by the vision of our
Founder Chancellor, Dr. T. R. Paarivendhar
, SRMIST aims to nurture excellence both in academics and athletics. The University offers
world-class sports facilities
including spacious playgrounds, indoor stadiums, fitness centers, and the state-of-the-art Dr. T. R. Paarivendhar Aquatic Complex. With professional coaching, modern infrastructure, and structured training programs, SRMIST encourages students to excel in national and international tournaments.
Annual intercollegiate events and intramural competitions foster teamwork, leadership, and sportsmanship across all disciplines. Through Sports Quota admissions and scholarships, talented athletes are recognized and supported to achieve their dreams. At SRMIST, sports are not just recreational activities—they are a way of life that builds discipline, determination, and character, truly reflecting the Founder Chancellor’s vision of empowering youth through education and sport.

Director - Sports
Sports play an important role in every human being’s day-to-day life and bring people together irrespective of their age, gender and nationality. Our Founder Chancellor has a great vision to make SRMIST as a Temple of Sports in India. Directorate of Sports is marching towards fulfilling his vision as a reality. Over the last few years, SRMIST has seen a lot of success like never before. Young achievers in various sports have won prizes and medals at various state, National and international level events. Our goal is to increase the participation of our teams in various competitions and win medals and bring laurels to the University and the country.
SRMIST is one of the few Universities in the country which encourages sports and games which has changed the lives of many young sports men and women. I strongly believe that sports help to inculcate teamwork, sacrifice, discipline and leadership qualities.
In addition to the regular sports activities, the Directorate of Sports, SRMIST offers a variety of courses through the Department of Physical Education & Sports Sciences and the Department of Yoga.

International Standard Swimming Pool
SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) boasts an international standard swimming pool at its Kattankulathur campus. This Olympic-sized facility, measuring 50 meters in length, is equipped with multiple lanes and advanced filtration systems to ensure a top-notch swimming experience. It serves as a hub for competitive training, recreational activities, and campus events, promoting a culture of fitness and well-being among students. With safety personnel on-site, the pool offers a secure environment for both professional athletes and casual swimmers.

Outdoor Games
Most popular sports like basketball, vollyball, football, cricket, tennis, kabbadi, badminton are played by a large number of students in the evenings as well as early morning in our beautiful sprawling outdoor grounds and courts.
Indoor Stadiums
Indoor sports activity is vibrant and our students have access to a vast range of indoor sports like Table Tennis, Shuttle, and other fitness activitis.
Fitness Center
For students to stay fit and healthy, all hostels are equipped with gym and fitness centre facilities – treadmill, exercise bikes and more of the latest fitness equipment.
The central gym for men at SRM is also equipped with body building equipment.
All gyms and fitness centers are open both in the morning and evening hours.
A healthy body and a healthy mind with Yoga
100s of students and staff of SRM Institute of Science and Technology (SRMIST) participated in the virtual celebration of International Day of Yoga. Large scale Yoga programs are held in the many facilities within the large sprawling campus of SRMIST including in its multipurpose indoor stadium, lawns, playgrounds, auditoriums. Renowned guests and Yoga specialists often visit our campus every year to help our students and staff master the ancient Indian gift to the world’s wellbeing and health. There is also students run Yoga club within SRMIST
Sporting Achievements
SRM Institute of Science and Technology values the inspiration and accomplishments that sports enable for our students and our institution. It is with this unflinching conviction that we are dedicated to scaling up our facilities, support, funding, and commitment towards excellence in sports.
We don’t believe in living on past laurels alone. We take them as proof of our potential for the future. Our constant and consistent victories of our students in various championships across the nation and internationally keep us forging ahead in this direction to foster excellence. It is our dedicated champion coaches and support staff with SRMIST’s leadership who bring out the best in our hard-working and committed sportspersons/students.
